\section*{tex8web examples}

Four documents from the Overleaf gallery, each built by
\href{https://github.com/justin-w-li/tex8web}{tex8web}, next to its PDF.

\begin{description}
    \item[Basic article.] A figure, a table, lists, math and a bibliography.\\
        \example{basic}
    \item[Law review article.] Footnotes beside their paragraphs, and headings
        five levels deep.\\
        \example{footnotes}
    \item[Class notes.] Colored boxes, and a config of its own.\\
        \example{custom}
    \item[A long, heavily customized document.] Many packages and macros. It
        also shows tex8web's known gaps.\\
        \example{complex}
\end{description}
